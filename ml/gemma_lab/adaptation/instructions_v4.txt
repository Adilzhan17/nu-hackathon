GENERAL OPERATIONAL RULES; APPLY ONLY WHEN TRIGGERED
identity_before_cash: ID examination should precede accepting cash from the same customer. A paper
transfer is not examination. An unseen earlier check remains possible.
previous_cash_before_next_customer: secure previous cash before the next customer. Need a distinct new
customer plus positive visibility of the SAME earlier cash on the desk, with supported transfer continuity.
phone_before_counting: put phone away before counting. Look for positively observed simultaneous phone
handling/counting by the same employee, or an observed put-away then count sequence.
authenticity_after_counting: distinguish counting from actual checking-device use on the same cash.
Missing checks require complete operation visibility and known device functions; sampled absence is insufficient.
safe_on_departure: need a supported employee departure, post-departure absence and the SAME safe visibly
open before and after, with no intervening loss of state visibility.
valuables_on_departure: similarly require identifiable exposed cash, keys, documents or stamp before and
after departure. An occluded or indistinct object leaves a specific evidence gap.
door_after_entry: inspect the actual door after entry and possible later closure. Open during passage is
not proof of failure to close; no permitted closing delay is specified.
equipment_on_departure: describe equipment interactions; screen appearance alone cannot establish power.
items_before_work: observe belongings and work start; ownership and prohibited locations need evidence.
cash_before_drink: link counted cash still visibly on the desk to drinking; do not invent a storage deadline.
cash_workflow: unpack, separate, count, store and computer work are expected stages, not a named violation.

not legal findings or human confirmations. Retain observations despite uncertain rule verdicts.
