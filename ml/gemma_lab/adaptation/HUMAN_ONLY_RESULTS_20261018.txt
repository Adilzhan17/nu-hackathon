Reproduction and audit locations
-------------------------------
Preparation:
  python -m ml.gemma_lab.adaptation.prepare_human_only \
    --run-root runs/gemma_lab/adaptation_v1 --output NEW/prepared \
    --repeats 1 --learning-rate 0.00005
For v3 use --repeats 2 --learning-rate 0.0001. Never overwrite the actual inputs.
Training uses adaptation.train --dataset <prepared/training_samples.jsonl>
--run-root runs/gemma_lab/adaptation_v1 --model artifacts/gemma_adaptation/base
--output NEW/train --max-updates 35 (v3:70) --max-seconds 900
--learning-rate 0.00005 (v3:0.0001) --save-optimizer.

run_eval uses prepared/eval_action_samples.jsonl, the frozen protocol, the
candidate's final checkpoint, --partition inner_holdout --task action
--seconds 900 --conditions base_v2prompt lora_v2prompt.

--partition inner_holdout --seconds 300. Both own the common GPU lock.

What is needed next
-------------------
Collect and manually verify before/during/after sequences for keys, stamps,
safe access and employee departure, including matched normal cases. Cover small
objects, occlusion and the alternative camera angle. Label visible objects,
actions and time boundaries separately from the legal finding. Sparse binary
video labels do not establish those event labels. Internal rules can refine
additional obligations when available; do not invent them in the meantime.
Freeze a new untouched test set before further tuning. Evaluate the actual
deployed GGUF end-to-end with both missed violations and false alarms before
claiming production readiness or an accuracy gain.
